# Content Review

Review a draft for factual accuracy, clarity, structure, tone, audience fit and UX writing, then return a publication recommendation and an improved version. The full workflow, with criteria and examples, is in `editing-and-quality/ai-content-review.md`.

## Before you start

Ask only for what is missing and needed:
- content type and channel,
- target audience and goal,
- tone of voice or brand guidelines,
- confirmed facts or source materials,
- for interface copy: what exactly happens after the call to action.

If facts or sources are not provided, continue, but treat every factual claim as unverified.

## Steps

1. Read the whole draft before commenting.
2. List every factual, numerical, legal, medical or technical claim. Mark each as **supported by the provided sources**, **unsupported by the provided sources** or **requires external verification**. Never treat your own knowledge as a verified source.
3. Find the three most important issues. For each: what is wrong, why it matters, the fix, and priority (high, medium, low).
4. For interface copy: check buttons, labels and error messages. Flag vague CTAs, inconsistent terms and manipulative or high-pressure wording. Check that the consequences of accepting, declining or cancelling are clear.
5. Rewrite the draft. Keep the meaning, the brand terms and any required legal wording. Add no facts, promises, statistics or features.

## Output

1. Overall assessment ending with one of: Ready for publication / Ready after minor revisions / Requires substantial revision / Not ready for publication.
2. Priority issues table.
3. Claims requiring verification, with evidence status.
4. UX writing recommendations, or "Not applicable".
5. Improved version.
6. Checklist marked Ready / Requires review / Not applicable.

## Rules

- Separate factual problems from stylistic suggestions.
- Explain significant changes instead of rewriting silently.
- Do not infer product features, account requirements or steps that were not given.
- Do not reproduce personal or confidential data.
- The result is a recommendation. A human approves publication.
